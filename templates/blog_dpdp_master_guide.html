Executive Summary (TL;DR)

  • Every Indian clinic, pathology lab, polyclinic, and hospital processing digital patient records is legally classified as a Data Fiduciary.

2. Legal Roles: Data Fiduciaries vs. Data Principals

To understand your clinic's legal exposure, you must master the statutory definitions defined by the Act:

  • Data Fiduciary (Your Clinic / Lab): Any person or legal entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data.
  • Data Principal (The Patient): The individual to whom the personal health data relates.
  • Data Processor (Your EMR / Software Vendor): Third-party software providers (such as cloud EHR software, SMS gateways, or accounting tools).
