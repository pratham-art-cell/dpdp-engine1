WhatsApp Medical Reports: Data Leakage Vulnerabilities Under the DPDP Act

Published: August 19, 2026 • 6 Min Read • Cybersecurity Review

Over 80% of standalone Indian pathology labs dispatch test results directly via personal WhatsApp accounts. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, this workflow creates severe legal and financial vulnerability for the diagnostic center.

The 3 Critical Compliance Failures

1. Endpoint Security Breaches (Section 8)

Staff phones storing patient PDFs on unencrypted local file systems create unmonitored attack vectors and unlogged data leakage points.

2. Absence of Audit Trails

Standard WhatsApp chats cannot provide tamper-proof evidentiary logs required during NABH or statutory data protection audits.

3. Section 33 Penalty Exposure

Failure to take reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches carries statutory penalties up to ₹250 Crores.
