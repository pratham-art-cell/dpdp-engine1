WhatsApp Medical Reports: Data Leakage Vulnerabilities Under the DPDP Act

Why transmitting unencrypted diagnostic PDFs across personal staff devices violates Section 8 reasonable safeguards and triggers multi-crore liability exposure under Section 33.

1. Endpoint Security Failures on Staff Devices

When staff dispatch reports from personal phones, diagnostic PDFs are stored in unencrypted device folders and backed up to consumer cloud storage accounts. This constitutes an uncontrolled data flow under Section 8.

2. Compliant Dispatch Architecture

To remain compliant, clinics must utilize automated API gateways that send one-time, password-protected download links rather than raw PDF attachments.
