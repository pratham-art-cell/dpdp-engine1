WhatsApp Medical Reports: The Biggest DPDP Risk for Clinics

In India, WhatsApp is the default communication channel. Patients expect their blood test PDFs and radiology reports to be delivered directly to their phones. However, under the DPDP Act, using unmanaged personal WhatsApp accounts to transmit Sensitive Personal Data is a massive liability.

The "Shadow IT" Danger

When a lab technician uses their personal mobile device to send a patient's HIV test or oncology scan, that data gets backed up to personal Google Drive or iCloud accounts. Under Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act, failing to prevent this unauthorized data sprawl is considered a failure of "reasonable security safeguards."

How to Comply Legally

You do not have to abandon WhatsApp, but you must change the architecture:

  • Explicit Consent: You must log verified consent indicating the patient specifically opted into WhatsApp delivery.
  • Enterprise API: Reports must be routed through the official WhatsApp Business API, ensuring endpoint encryption and access logging, not through a staff member's personal app.
