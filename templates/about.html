Company Overview

Securing India's Digital Health Infrastructure

ConsentLayer is a dedicated regulatory technology platform built exclusively to help Indian clinics and pathology labs navigate the complexities of the DPDP Act 2023.

⚖️

Our Mission

Enterprise hospitals have armies of lawyers. Standalone clinics and diagnostic centers do not. We level the playing field by providing automated, enterprise-grade Section 33 audit logging and Section 5 consent generation to SME healthcare providers at a fraction of the cost.

🛡️

Technical Integrity

We believe patient data privacy is a fundamental right. Our platform utilizes strict access-control algorithms, immutable cryptographic logging, and zero-knowledge architectures to ensure that sensitive diagnostic files are never exposed to unauthorized endpoints.

Regulatory Affairs & Support Desk

Operating from Ratlam, Madhya Pradesh, our technical implementation team is ready to assist your clinic with custom LIMS integrations.
