Company Overview

Securing India's Digital Health Infrastructure

ConsentLayer is a dedicated regulatory technology platform built exclusively to help Indian clinics and pathology labs navigate the technical mandates of the DPDP Act 2023.

⚖️

Our Mission

Enterprise hospitals have legal divisions. Standalone clinics and diagnostic centers do not. We level the playing field by providing automated Section 8 audit logging and Section 5 consent notice generation to SME healthcare providers.

🛡️

Technical Safeguards

We implement DPDP Section 8 obligations using strict access controls, cryptographic access logging, and tokenized report endpoints to prevent unconsented exposure of patient health information.

Regulatory Affairs & Technical Implementation

Operating from Ratlam, Madhya Pradesh, our technical engineering team assists diagnostic centers with compliant LIMS integrations.
