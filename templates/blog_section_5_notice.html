DPDP Section 5 Notice Requirements for Pathology Labs & Diagnostic Centers

Published: August 19, 2026 • 5 Min Read • Legal & Technical Review

Under Section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, every healthcare entity collecting patient data must provide an itemized, clear notice prior to or at the time of seeking consent.

Mandatory Elements of a Compliant Section 5 Notice

  • Specific Data Categories: Detailed enumeration of biometric, demographic, and medical test parameters being processed.
  • Specified Purpose: Clear purpose limitation solely for diagnostic testing, billing, and report dispatch.
  • Exercise of Rights: Direct pathways for the patient to access, correct, or withdraw consent at any time.
  • Grievance Redressal: Name and direct contact credentials of your clinic's designated Data Protection Officer (DPO).
  • Board Complaints: Instructions on how the patient can file complaints with the Data Protection Board of India.

Multilingual Notice Provision
