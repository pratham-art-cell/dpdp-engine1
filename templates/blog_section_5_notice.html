DPDP Section 5 Notice Requirements for Pathology Labs & Diagnostic Centers

A practical guide to drafting itemized data processing notices, implementing 8th Schedule multilingual workflows, and eliminating bundled consent liabilities.

1. The Mandate for Multilingual Clear Notices

Section 5(1) mandates that every healthcare facility must provide an explicit notice before or at the time of collecting patient information. The notice must specify the categories of data collected and provide a direct method for consent withdrawal.

2. Prohibiting Bundled Consent

Consent cannot be coerced as a condition for receiving standard medical care. Pre-ticked checkboxes and opaque blanket privacy policies are explicitly non-compliant under DPDP rules.
