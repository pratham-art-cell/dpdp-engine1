DPDP Section 5 Notice Requirements for Indian Pathology Labs

When a patient walks into a diagnostic center, handing over an Aadhaar card and a phone number is standard practice. Under the DPDP Act, collecting this data without a Section 5 Compliant Notice is a direct regulatory violation.

The Multilingual Mandate

Section 5 of the DPDP Act states that the Data Principal (the patient) must have the option to view the privacy notice in English or any of the 22 languages specified in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution. A Hindi-speaking patient in Madhya Pradesh cannot be legally bound by an English-only consent form.

Itemization of Purpose

Your notice must explicitly state:

  • What personal data is being collected (e.g., blood group, phone number).
  • The exact purpose of processing (e.g., generating diagnostic reports vs. marketing).
  • The contact details of your clinic's Data Protection Officer or grievance redressal mechanism.
