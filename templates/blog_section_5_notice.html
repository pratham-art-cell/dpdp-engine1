DPDP Section 5 Notice Requirements for Pathology Labs & Diagnostic Centers

A practical guide to drafting itemized data processing notices, implementing 8th Schedule multilingual workflows, and eliminating bundled consent liabilities.

Notice Requirements Summary

  • Under Section 5(1), every healthcare provider must serve an explicit, itemized notice before or at the time of collecting patient data.
  • Notices must be presented in English or any of the 22 scheduled languages under the 8th Schedule of the Indian Constitution.
  • Serving invalid notices exposes clinics to penalties of up to ₹50 Crores under Section 33.

1. The Legal Mandate of Section 5 in Clinical Settings

In standard clinical practice, patient data intake is rapid. Reception desks collect contact details, government identification, and sample identifiers without informing patients of data flows. Under Section 5, this practice is non-compliant.

2. Mandatory Components of a Valid Section 5 Notice

To satisfy DPBI regulatory standards, every notice served at OPD registration counters or diagnostic lab portals must contain five itemized elements:

  • Specific Data Categories: Complete disclosure of demographic details and biometric markers.
  • Specified Purpose: Clear limitation stating the data is processed solely for conducting tests.
  • Manner of Exercising Rights: Instructions explaining how the patient can access their records or withdraw consent.
